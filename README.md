## 📋 About openlink

openlink lets your web-based AI directly access files on your computer and run local commands. This means you can use AI tools through your web browser while they read, write, or manage files where you store your documents, pictures, or projects. openlink is designed for regular users who want a faster way to combine AI with files on their PC. You don't need to know code or change your system deeply. openlink acts like a bridge between your local files and the AI you use online.

## 📂 How openlink Works

openlink runs in the background on Windows. It listens for requests from web-based AI software. When the AI asks to open, read, or modify a file, openlink performs that action locally, then sends back the results.

Because of this method, the AI never needs full access to your PC. openlink accepts limited commands for safety and ease.
